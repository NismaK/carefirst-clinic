How it works

Four simple steps from request to follow-up.

  1. Send a request

    Share your name, phone number, preferred date and a short reason.

  2. We confirm

    Our front desk gets in touch to agree on a time.

  3. Meet your clinician

    Visit in person or join by video.

  4. Plan next steps

    Leave with a clear plan and any follow-up arranged.
